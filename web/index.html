    How this works

    StepDone byHow to read it
    Identify the gene; fetch assemblies, orthologs, GO/KEGG/UniProt annotations; search expression datasets Programmatic lookups — NCBI, OMA, UniProt, KEGG, GEO, Expression Atlas Facts straight from the source DBs — take at face value (but only as complete as those DBs)
    Pick the right record when several match; score every edge 0–1; rank datasets JEV judge (TypeSafe.ai) sees candidates + evidence Confidence = judge's plausibility score — good for ranking, not a measured error rate
    Draft claims from node text; write the summary (Full analysis only) LLM proposes → JEV verifies each claim Grain of salt — only nodes the judge scores ≥ 0.5 reach the LLM; claims scoring < 0.5 are dropped — but kept claims can still be wrong

    Fine print

    • Confidence is a score, not a probability — use it to triage, not to conclude.
    • absent_in edges mean "no ortholog found" — could be annotation gaps, not true absence.
    • Everything fetched appears in the graph — caps only limit what gets judged or summarized: orthologs → top 100 by OMA score; datasets → top 250 (exact species first, then JEV score); AI claims → top 100 datasets + 100 publications by triage score, ≤ 12 claims each.
    • Texts sent to the judge/LLM are truncated (600–8000 chars) — a low score can mean the interesting bit was cut off.
    • Click a node for its evidence trail and source links; hover for context; the edges/nodes tables are the same data in tabular form.
